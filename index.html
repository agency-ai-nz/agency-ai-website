Why Agency AI

NZ's public sector has 272 AI use cases across 70 agencies — double the year before. The ambition is there. The capacity to execute isn't.

Most agencies are stuck with Copilot and a data infrastructure from 2014. The gap between policy intent and working technology keeps widening.

Agency AI closes that gap. We build agentic tools that work — not strategy decks, not pilots that go nowhere.

Our work is grounded in deep public-sector experience: Treasury's Living Standards Framework, energy and environment policy, and years of building AI for public benefit.

We know what agencies need, how procurement works, and how to make AI actually land.

How we work

Build, not slides

We deliver working agentic tools. Every engagement ends with something in production — not a report recommending the next engagement.

Reusable by design

Agents built for one agency can be adapted across the sector. Your investment scales — the next agency doesn't start from zero.

Outcomes first

Every project starts with the outcome, not the technology. We measure success the way agencies do — by what changes in the world.
