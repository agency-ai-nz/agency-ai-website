How we work

Outcomes first

Before we build anything, we map where AI genuinely helps your people — scoring the opportunities by impact and feasibility, and ranking what to do first.

A team inside your team

We work alongside your people, not as consultants who hand over a report. We transfer the capability to run and extend what we build — the goal is for you not to need us.

Working tools, not slideshows

We ship real AI automation into your day-to-day operations, built on the tools you already have. Not strategy decks. Not slide presentations. Things that work.

One loop. Deeper every pass.

We run a structured methodology alongside your organisation — finding the highest-value AI opportunities, building the tools to deliver them, and proving the outcomes. Each pass goes deeper.

01

Discovery

Map how your work really flows: what's repetitive, what's costly, where the biggest AI returns are. A structured picture before any build.

02

Opportunities

Surface the top opportunities. Each one gets a plain-language case: what it would do, what it would take, what it would return.

03

Implementation

Commission the build. We work inside your environment, using the tools you already have, and transfer the capability as we go.

04

Evaluation

Track delivery against the baseline. The results are real — not a forecast we forget once the engagement ends.

Who we work with

Public sector

Central and local government: policy, operations and service-delivery teams. Resource-constrained and security-conscious, but ready for something that actually works.

AI Opportunity Audit

Our front door. A structured discovery that maps your highest-value AI opportunities and tells you what to build first.

Consultation analysis

Auto-read and analyse thousands of public submissions. Reduce the manual burden of legislated reporting by up to 30%.

OIA response drafting

Draft OIA responses from your agency's documents, flag gaps, and track deadlines. Replicable to every agency.

Policy Q&A

Grounded, referenced answers on your agency's own documents. No hallucination — every answer cites its source.

Briefings & reports

Auto-draft ministerial briefings, Cabinet papers and committee reports from source documents. Your team reviews and signs off.

Case triage & routing

Classify and route incoming requests, complaints and cases automatically. Free your frontline team for the work that needs a human.

Community sector

NGOs, community organisations, iwi and social-service providers. High-value work, but there is always too much to do. AI can triple your team's impact.

AI Readiness Programme

A lighter on-ramp for smaller organisations. Workshops, a readiness assessment, and a plain-language roadmap for what to do next.

Fundraising tool

AI-assisted grant writing and funder reporting. Spend less time on applications, more time on the work that matters.

Impact reporting

Generate funder and board reports automatically from your programme data. Prove your impact without adding admin hours.

Comms & social media

Draft newsletters, social posts and stakeholder updates from your programme work. Stay visible without the time cost.

Volunteer management

Automate scheduling, onboarding and communications for volunteer programmes. More capacity for the work your volunteers came to do.

Research tool

A research agent that monitors policy, funding and sector news and briefs your team weekly. Stay across what matters without the hours.
