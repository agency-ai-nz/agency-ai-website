Why Agency AI

Government agencies and community organisations are committed to improve outcomes for New Zealanders. AI can help you achieve that, at pace, and Agency AI is here to make that happen.

Public sector agencies are doing their best with Copilot licences and data infrastructure from 2014. Community organisations are doing high-value work with almost no AI capability at all. In both cases, ambition runs well ahead of execution.

The dominant AI narrative is about efficiency and job-cutting. We think that misses the point. AI should help your organisation do more of what it exists to do.

When we walk into a room, we bring a working demo against your real problem. Not a deck. Not a proposal. Something that works.

How we work

Build, not slides

We build software your team can use from day one. Every engagement ends with something working, not a report recommending the next engagement.

Reusable by design

Tools built for one organisation can be adapted across the sector. Your investment goes further. The next organisation doesn't start from zero.

Outcomes first

We start by asking what outcomes matter to you, then build toward those. Every engagement begins with one question: what do you want to be different?

What we build

We work across two sectors, each with different constraints and different opportunities. Everything we deliver is working software, not a report.

Public sector

Central and local government agencies: policy teams, operations teams, service delivery teams. Resource-constrained, risk-averse, and ready for something that actually works.

Consultation analysis

Synthesises public feedback from LTPs, district plans, and statutory consultations at scale. Already validated with real clients.

OIA response drafting

Drafts responses from agency documents, flags missing information, and tracks deadlines so nothing slips.

Policy Q&A

A bot trained on your agency's own documents. Staff ask questions and get grounded, referenced answers instantly.

Briefings and reports

Automates routine ministerial briefings, Cabinet papers, and internal reports from structured inputs.

AI Opportunity Audit

A structured discovery process that maps where AI agents can save your team time. The right starting point before any build begins.

Case triage and routing

Categorises and routes incoming requests, complaints, or applications to the right team automatically, cutting handling time and reducing errors.

Community sector

NGOs, community organisations, iwi entities, and social service providers. High-value work, limited capacity. AI can change that ratio significantly.

Fundraising tool

Populates grant and funding applications automatically from a single source of truth about your programmes and outcomes.

Impact reporting

Generates funder reports and stakeholder updates from programme data, formatted to each funder's requirements.

Comms and social media

Drafts newsletters, media releases, and social content from briefs or structured inputs, aligned to your brand voice.

Volunteer management

Handles recruitment, rostering, communications, and recognition workflows so your team can focus on the work that matters.

AI Readiness Programme

A lightweight facilitated programme for organisations not yet ready to commission a build. Includes AI literacy, a readiness assessment, and a practical roadmap.

Research tool

Continuously collects, summarises, and surfaces relevant information: funding opportunities, sector news, policy developments, and evidence updates tailored to your focus areas.
