How we work

Outcomes first

Before we build anything, Clarity maps where AI genuinely helps your people: scoring the opportunities by impact and feasibility, and ranking what to do first.

A team inside your team

We work alongside your people, not as consultants who hand over a report. We transfer the capability to run and extend what we build.

Working tools, not slideshows

We ship real AI automation into your day-to-day operations, built on the tools you already have. Not strategy decks. Not slide presentations.

One loop. Deeper every pass.

Clarity, our AI management platform, runs the loop alongside your organisation: finding the highest-value AI opportunities, building the tools, and proving the outcomes.

01

Discovery

Clarity reads the public record and the documents you share, mapping how the work really runs: what's repetitive, what's costly, where the returns are.

02

Opportunities

Clarity drafts a plain-language case for every opportunity: what it would do, what it would take, and what it would return. You refine it until it's right.

03

Implementation

Commission the build. Clarity gives every committed project a named owner, a team and a connected folder, and we transfer the capability as we go.

04

Evaluation

Clarity tracks each project against the measures its owner agreed, automatically. Real results, not a forecast we forget once the engagement ends.

Who we work with

Central government

Ministries and central agencies: policy, regulatory and service-delivery teams. Security-conscious, but ready for something that actually works.

Clarity · AI Opportunity Audit

Our front door. Clarity reads your public record and the documents you share, then ranks your highest-value AI opportunities and tells you what to build first.

Ministerial & OIA drafting

Draft OIA responses and ministerial correspondence from your own documents, flag the gaps, and track every statutory deadline.

Case notes & records assist

Give caseworkers their week back: draft case notes and assemble records, privacy-safe, with every judgement staying human.

Briefings & Cabinet papers

Auto-draft ministerial briefings, Cabinet papers and committee reports from source documents. Your team reviews and signs off.

Consultation & submission analysis

Read and analyse thousands of public submissions, grouped by theme, with every claim traced back to its source.

Monitoring & statutory reporting

Compile recurring monitoring and statutory reports from source data automatically. Cuts a multi-FTE task down to hours.

Local government

Councils and local authorities delivering front-line services to communities. Same constraints as central government, same appetite for tools that actually work.

Clarity · AI Opportunity Audit

Our front door. Clarity maps how your council's work actually runs, then ranks the highest-value AI opportunities and tells you what to build first.

LGOIMA response drafting

Draft LGOIMA responses from your council's own documents, flag gaps, and track deadlines. Replicable to every council.

Consents & LIM assembly

Pre-check completeness and assemble LIMs and consent files from council records. Faster turnaround, and the officer still decides.

Service requests & triage

Classify and route incoming requests, complaints and cases automatically. Free your frontline for the work that needs a human.

Statutory reporting & compliance

Assemble the recurring statutory and compliance reporting small councils carry with no slack in the roster.

Contact centre & enquiry assist

Handle routine calls and requests automatically, and give your team the context to resolve the rest faster.

Community sector

NGOs, community organisations, iwi and social-service providers. High-value work, but there is always too much to do. AI can triple your team's impact.

Clarity · AI Readiness

A lighter on-ramp for smaller organisations. Clarity builds the picture, and we add workshops and a plain-language roadmap for what to do next.

Grant & funder reporting

Impact & partner reporting

Generate funder, board and partner reports automatically from what your programmes already record, without adding admin hours.

Enquiry triage & navigation

Volunteer rostering & allocation

Knowledge & answer assist
