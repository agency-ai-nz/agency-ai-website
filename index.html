One platform. One continuous loop.

Agency isn't a one-off project or a slide deck. It's a platform that runs a continuous loop alongside your organisation — going deeper each pass, and building your team's capability as it goes.

01

Sense

Build an honest, evidence-based picture of how the work flows today — from the public record and the documents your team points us to.

02

Develop

Surface the opportunities where AI genuinely helps, and develop each into a current-state picture, a plan and a business case.

03

Commit

Weigh the options and commit the ones worth doing — with the capacity they free tallied up in real, measurable terms.

04

Monitor

Track delivery against the baseline and the forecast — measured automatically from your own systems, with people always in the loop.

05

Outcomes

Reinvest the capacity you've freed into the public good — and show the real results you've produced along the way.

Built for the public sector

Reads systems, not people

Least-privilege, read-only access to the documents and systems you choose. Your people volunteer what they know — Agency never surveils them.

Forecasts, then proves it

Every recommendation comes with a forecast return. Agency then tracks the real result against it, so the value is never just a promise.

Sovereign and secure by default

Runs inside your own Microsoft and Azure environment, under your security model. Your data and your agency stay in New Zealand.

And a human is always in the loop. Agency drafts, measures and reports — but your people review, correct and sign off before anything is lodged. Nothing happens behind their backs.

Outcomes without more budget

The point isn't cutting jobs — it's freeing time. Agency takes the repetitive work off your team's plate and measures the capacity it frees in real terms. You decide where it goes: faster consents, better services, the community work you've never had time for. Better outcomes, delivered without asking for more money.
