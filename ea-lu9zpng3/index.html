THE PUBLIC GOOD

The first app, free

Clarity: where AI and automation would help most, with the evidence attached

Clarity builds a Discovery of your organisation: how its work flows, where the effort goes, and which improvements are worth making. Every claim links to the passage it came from, so it stands up to checking.

  1. 1

    Sources

    Annual and quarterly reports, consultations and reviews, and later your own documents and conversations.

  2. 2

    Figures

    Headline numbers, each for a named period, traceable to where they came from.

  3. 3

    Services

    The work people come to you for: its volume, mandate and cost, and how far it could be automated.

  4. 4

    Insights

    Findings, strengths, risks and public sentiment, each about a specific service.

  5. 5

    Opportunities

    Improvements to named services, resting on the insights behind them, with the strength of evidence rated.
